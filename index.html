01 / Apple · Fundamentals

Services improves earnings quality, but valuation leaves little room for error

Investment summary

Apple's June-quarter 2026 net sales reached $109.4bn, up 16% year on year. The growth mix was led by iPhone (+22%) and Services (+12%), while Mac also expanded strongly. This keeps the ecosystem flywheel intact: installed-device activity supports recurring Services revenue, while premium hardware remains the largest revenue engine.

The quality of the mix matters as much as the top line. Services carried a 75.6% gross margin versus 40.1% for Products in Q3 2026. A larger Services contribution therefore supports margin resilience and raises the value of customer retention, cross-selling and ecosystem engagement.

The offset is valuation. At a 30 September market close of $333.02, the stock already reflects substantial confidence in long-run cash-flow growth and strategic optionality. The following valuation work is therefore framed as a sensitivity exercise, not a recommendation.

02 / USD/JPY · Macro / FX

Carry persists, but policy convergence is becoming the dominant risk

Exhibit 4 · USD/JPY and U.S.-Japan 2Y yield differential

Our read: the absolute spread still rewards holding dollars, but direction increasingly depends on where markets expect the front-end differential to move, not on today's carry alone.

U.S. side. The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75-4.00% in September while describing inflation as elevated. August CPI was 3.4% year on year. That combination keeps the short end sensitive to inflation and labour-market surprises rather than guaranteeing a smooth easing path.

Japan side. The Bank of Japan's current guideline is to encourage the uncollateralised overnight call rate around 1.25%. A faster normalisation path would pull Japanese front-end yields higher and compress the carry advantage that has supported USD/JPY.

Positioning implication. A high spot level by itself is not a short thesis. The relevant question is whether policy expectations compress the front-end spread quickly enough to change hedging demand and carry economics.

02 / USD/JPY · Scenario framework

The debate is the future spread, not today's carry

A macro thesis becomes useful only when it specifies what would make it wrong.
ScenarioRates pathFX implicationKey trigger
JPY-positiveSpread narrows fasterUSD/JPY lowerFaster BOJ normalisation, softer U.S. inflation / growth, or a lower expected Fed path

USD-positiveSpread widens / stays wider for longerUSD/JPY higherU.S. inflation reprices the Fed path upward or Japan normalises more slowly than expected

Transmission mechanism

1Policy expectations reprice U.S. and Japanese front-end yields.

2Yield differential changes the economics of carry and FX hedging.

3Flows and positioning affect how strongly USD/JPY responds to the same macro surprise.

4Spot price is the output; the thesis should be invalidated by the underlying macro mechanism, not by noise alone.

Catalysts to monitor

  • U.S. CPI, payrolls and Fed communication.
  • BOJ meetings, wage/inflation evidence and JGB repricing.
  • Changes in the 2Y yield spread and options-implied volatility.
  • Official commentary around excessive FX moves or intervention risk.

Invalidation discipline

Rather than anchoring on one target, the thesis is reassessed when the expected policy path, spread direction or catalyst sequence changes materially.

Design principles

  • Predefine invalidation before entry. A thesis should have a condition under which it no longer holds.
  • Size from risk, not conviction. Position size is a separate decision from directional confidence.
  • Separate outcome from process. A profitable trade can still be a poor decision; a losing trade can still follow a sound framework.
  • Backtest rule changes before adoption. Process changes should be tested against prior data rather than accepted because of one recent outcome.

Methodology / Sources

Research approach and primary sources

Framework

Thesis → Evidence → Catalysts → Invalidation → Review. The aim is to make assumptions visible, connect them to observable evidence and state what would change the conclusion. Valuation and macro outputs are simplified educational models designed to demonstrate analytical process.
